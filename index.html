How the Ackerman method works

Four shrinking offers make every concession feel like a real sacrifice.

  1. 65% anchor: an extreme opening that resets expectations
  2. 85%: a big move that shows flexibility
  3. 95%: a smaller move, your budget is tightening
  4. 100% + perk: your real number plus a non-cash extra
Sellers mirror it: 135%, 115%, 105%, 100%. Between offers, use calibrated questions like “How am I supposed to do that?” and let silence work.
Method from Chris Voss, Never Split the Difference
