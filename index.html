A non-cash concession is something extra you offer that doesn't cost you money but adds value — like paying today, faster handoff, free setup, or a warranty. It signals you've hit your financial limit while still moving the deal forward.
Buyer ladder: 65% → 85% → 95% → Final
The Ackerman model uses four tapering offers to make your counterpart feel every concession is a final sacrifice.
  1. 65% anchor — extreme opening that resets expectations
  2. 85% step — large move showing flexibility
  3. 95% step — smaller move signalling budget is tightening
  4. 100% final + perk — your real target plus a non-cash extra
Decreasing concession sizes create psychological exhaustion. Odd-number pricing signals a calculated, firm limit. Method popularised by Chris Voss in Never Split the Difference.
